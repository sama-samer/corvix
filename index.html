What Corvix means

Core, Intelligence, Vision.

Corvix is one platform built in three layers — each one solving a specific part of why brownfield factories stay disconnected.

C

Core

The unified data backbone. Connects legacy machines, PLCs, sensors, ERP, and manual records through a Unified Namespace built on MQTT and OPC UA — without replacing what's already running on the floor.

I

Intelligence

The AI decision engine. Every recommendation is grounded in your own factory's operational data through retrieval-augmented generation, so it explains its reasoning instead of asking for blind trust.

V

Vision

One current picture of the plant — maintenance, energy, production, and quality together, in Arabic or English, from the floor or from anywhere.

How it works

From scattered signals to a decision, in four steps.

01 / CONNECT

Connect what's already there

Brownfield connectivity to existing machines, PLCs, and sensors — no rip-and-replace required.

02 / UNIFY

Unify into one namespace

Every source feeds a single Unified Namespace, so the whole plant shares one live picture instead of five disconnected ones.

03 / DECIDE

Generate explainable decisions

The AI engine prioritizes recommendations across maintenance, energy, production, and quality — grounded in your data, not a generic model.

04 / ACT

Operators keep the call

Corvix launches with advisory functions first. It recommends. Your team decides.

Human-in-the-loop by design: Corvix recommends, operators decide — automation is introduced only after trust is earned on the floor.
